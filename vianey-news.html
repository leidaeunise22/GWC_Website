MEET VIANEY MARTINEZ

Girls Who Code College Loop at UTEP

Vianey Martinez is a Computer Science student at the University of Texas at El Paso and one of the most distinguished leaders of Girls Who Code College Loop. Her journey reflects excellence, resilience, and a deep commitment to empowering women in technology.

Vianey received the prestigious Hawkins Scholarship, the Student Leadership Award, interned at Microsoft, and won an AI Hackathon. These achievements highlight both her academic strength and her technical innovation. Beyond her accomplishments, her leadership is based on mentorship, service, and creating opportunities for others to grow.

Vianey’s story embodies what College Loop stands for: fostering confidence, cultivating leadership, and opening doors to meaningful impact in the tech world.
